# How to cite

Cite the paper. Cite this archive as well if you use the split, the prompts, the
per-query table, or the statistical script.

## The paper

    Sneiderman, R.: Pre-Deployment Stress Testing of Source-Grounded
    Conversational AI in Non-Imaging Mental-Health Contexts: A 100-Query,
    20-Risk-Category Methodology with Grounded-Refusal Contracts. In:
    Proceedings of the Fifth Workshop on Applications of Medical AI (AMAI 2026),
    MICCAI 2026 satellite event. Lecture Notes in Computer Science, Springer
    (2026).

BibTeX:

    @inproceedings{sneiderman2026stresstest,
      author    = {Sneiderman, Robert},
      title     = {Pre-Deployment Stress Testing of Source-Grounded
                   Conversational {AI} in Non-Imaging Mental-Health Contexts:
                   A 100-Query, 20-Risk-Category Methodology with
                   Grounded-Refusal Contracts},
      booktitle = {Applications of Medical AI (AMAI 2026), MICCAI 2026 Workshop},
      series    = {Lecture Notes in Computer Science},
      publisher = {Springer},
      year      = {2026}
    }

Fill in volume, pages, and DOI once Springer assigns them.

## This archive

    Sneiderman, R.: Reproducibility archive for "Pre-Deployment Stress Testing
    of Source-Grounded Conversational AI in Non-Imaging Mental-Health Contexts"
    (2026).

BibTeX:

    @misc{sneiderman2026archive,
      author = {Sneiderman, Robert},
      title  = {Reproducibility archive for {\emph{Pre-Deployment Stress Testing
                of Source-Grounded Conversational AI in Non-Imaging
                Mental-Health Contexts}}},
      year   = {2026},
      note   = {CC BY 4.0}
    }

Add `howpublished` with the repository URL, or `doi` if the archive is deposited
somewhere that mints one. A DOI is the better target for a citation in the
published paper, since it survives a repository being renamed or moved.

## What the archive is not

The numbers here describe one consumer deployment at one time point, evaluated
by one judge instance which is also the generating model, on a split that was
used during development of the component under test. They are a pre-deployment
contract check on that system. Do not cite them as a benchmark result, as a
model-quality comparison, or as evidence of clinical efficacy.
